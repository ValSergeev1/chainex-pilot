SERVICE OPTIONS & PROGRAM DESIGN

Define the service. Make the handoffs clear.

Oversized shipments require a delivery plan that considers the product and the destination together. A palletized order for a driveway and a boxed furniture item for an apartment can require different vehicles, handling and appointment arrangements.

  1. 01

    Curbside or front-door delivery

    Define the accepted unloading point and what the customer must arrange after the handoff. Review pallet, driveway, loading-zone and safe-access conditions.

  2. 02

    Room-of-choice placement

    Confirm the room, delivery path, door clearances, stairs, elevators and team-lift needs. Placement is separate from unpacking or assembly unless included.

  3. 03

    Appointment-based programs

    Align customer contact, delivery windows, site restrictions and the procedure for an unavailable recipient.

PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Before we get moving.

Is room of choice the same as white glove?

Room of choice covers agreed placement inside the delivery location. Assembly, unpacking, installation, debris removal, and haul-away are separate services unless expressly included.

Can every shipment use a van?

No. Dimensions, weight, handling, and site access determine whether a van, liftgate truck, or another configuration is appropriate.
