Common questions

Which vehicles can I use?

Bring a vehicle you own or have permission to use. Vehicle and equipment requirements depend on the route.

Where is work available?

Tell us which markets you can cover. ChainEx reviews applications against program needs in those markets.

What documents do I need?

Start with contact and operating details. If invited to complete setup, use the secure portal for requested documents.

How are rates and payment terms shared?
