WHAT COVERAGE MEANS FOR YOUR PROGRAM

Start with your delivery locations.

Delivery territory

Provide the delivery locations, residential or commercial mix and any remote or restricted-access destinations. City names alone may not define the operating area.

Equipment & handling

Shipment dimensions, weight, palletization, liftgate needs and crew requirements determine which configuration can serve the work.

Schedule & frequency

Receiving windows, delivery days, appointment needs and pickup deadlines shape route and lane availability.

Facility arrangements

A market on the map does not imply a warehouse in that city. Receiving, staging, cross-dock space and holding periods require program-specific confirmation.

Customer handoff

Curbside, front-door, room-of-choice and white-glove services need different preparation. Identify the required level before launch.

Exceptions & returns

Agree how access issues, failed attempts, return destinations and changes in volume will be handled within the service area.

COVERAGE QUESTIONS

Before you plan the next move.

Does the map show every location you can serve?

No. It shows representative markets. Share your pickup and delivery locations so we can review the requested coverage.

Are all services immediately available in every market?

The same service menu applies across the regional network, but crews, equipment, facility arrangements and service availability must be confirmed for the individual program.

Does nationwide cartage mean nationwide recurring parcel delivery?

Nationwide emergency and ad hoc requests are reviewed separately from recurring regional delivery programs. The shipment location, timing and available equipment determine whether an urgent move can be accepted.

Can you support nearby cities or suburban delivery areas?

Provide the delivery locations and shipment profile. We assess the proposed territory, route density, access conditions and required service rather than assuming a fixed radius around a city.

Can one program cover multiple markets?

We can review a coordinated program with market-specific coverage, equipment and schedules. Launch readiness, commercial terms and operational contacts are established before accepting volume.

What happens if my volume or territory changes?

Discuss the change with the operating team. Additional delivery locations, routes, service days or handling requirements are reviewed against available capacity and the accepted scope.
