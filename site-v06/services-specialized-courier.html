PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Before we get moving.

Does an inquiry confirm specialized capability?

No. We assess the actual requirements before accepting work. Do not assume specialized certifications, temperature control, or regulated handling from this website.

Should I include patient or sensitive information?

No. Keep sensitive records, patient data, identification documents, and protected information out of website forms.
