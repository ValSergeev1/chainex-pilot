FREQUENT SHIPPER QUESTIONS

Before you commit freight.

Do you support both recurring and one-time moves?

Yes. We scope dedicated programs and individual requests against available equipment, territory, shipment profile, and timing.

Are all services available in every ZIP?

No. The service menu is consistent across the regional network, but ZIP coverage, facilities, equipment, and handling are confirmed for each program.

Can you meet our integration requirements?

We review manifest, label, API, EDI, tracking, and proof-of-delivery requirements during onboarding. The implementation and timing must be confirmed.

What affects the price?

Volume, density, shipment profile, equipment, service level, access, appointments, operating schedule, and additional attempts all affect the quote.

How do I request time-critical support?

Call (206) 539-9549. Share exact locations, freight dimensions, weight, ready time, and deadline. Dispatch is confirmed only after capacity and terms are agreed.
