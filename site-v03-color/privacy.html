WEBSITE INQUIRIES

Your information.

When you submit a quote, partner inquiry, or contractor registration, Chain Express Global Logistics, LLC receives the contact, company, location, and operating details you provide. Requests and contractor profiles are stored in the website’s database for review by authorized ChainEx team members according to their assigned role. Contractor registrations are linked to your signed-in account identifier so you can view your own applications and their review status.

ChainEx account sign-in

Applicants and partners use an email verification code to access their ChainEx account. Codes expire after 10 minutes and can be used once. Codes are stored as protected hashes; session tokens are stored as hashes and use secure, HTTP-only cookies that expire after seven days. Request and verification limits help protect accounts from abuse. Sign-in emails are sent through ChainEx’s Google Workspace mailbox. No ChatGPT account is required for applicant or partner access. Team administration has separate access controls.

How we use it

We use inquiry details to respond, assess service requirements or partner fit, review capability profiles, show application status, prepare quotes, and coordinate onboarding. A form submission is not a booking or transportation contract.

Partner portal and documents

Signed-in partners can view profiles linked to their account and upload requested operational PDF documents. Files are stored privately and downloads require authorization. Internal notes and assignments are not displayed to partners. Team activity, including record changes and document downloads, is logged. Document expiration dates support reminders inside the management dashboard.

Team applications

Career applications collect business contact details, preferred roles and locations, availability, experience, skills and an optional professional PDF resume. They are linked to your signed-in account. The applicant can view their own application and resume; authorized owner and recruiting accounts can review the recruiting pool. Internal review notes and assignments are not displayed to applicants. Applications and resume downloads are recorded in the activity history. Status updates appear in the application view. A confirmation is sent to the verified sign-in email and an application alert is sent to ChainEx Support. Notification emails contain a reference and portal link, not sensitive documents.

Payment preferences

Account-linked partners can submit ACH account-holder, bank, account-type, routing and account details, or their registered Zelle email/mobile number. These preferences are stored separately from operational profiles and can be viewed by the account owner and authorized ChainEx owner/finance staff. Changes are logged by reference. Saving a preference does not verify banking ownership or initiate a payment. ACH details are encrypted before storage using a server-managed key held separately from the database. Normal views show only the account’s last four digits. Authorized detail views require a separate request and are logged. Banking information is not included in ordinary partner record exports, activity-log content or notification emails.

What to leave out

Keep tax identifiers, identity documents and banking information out of general inquiry forms, operational notes and email. Use the dedicated W-9, secure paperwork and payment sections only when requested and applicable to your relationship. Tax and secure paperwork are encrypted and restricted to your account and authorized ChainEx owner/finance users. Do not submit patient or medical information.
