WHAT A GOOD PARTNERSHIP REQUIRES

Clear expectations. Consistent execution.

We look for accurate capacity information, suitable equipment, safe operating practices, responsive dispatch communication, and the ability to follow the agreed handling and delivery requirements.

Rates, payment terms, responsibilities, documentation, service standards, and any program-specific requirements are addressed during onboarding and in the applicable agreement.

Does registration guarantee routes?

No. Registration creates a profile for review. Available work depends on market demand, fit, qualifications, onboarding, and a separate agreement.

Where do I send insurance or tax documents?

Do not upload them through the initial application. Our team coordinates the required documentation separately through the agreed onboarding process.

Can an individual driver register?

Yes. Select independent driver in the portal. DSP businesses and established carriers have separate registration categories.

Can I see the review status?

Sign in through the contractor portal using the same account you used to submit your ChainEx application.
