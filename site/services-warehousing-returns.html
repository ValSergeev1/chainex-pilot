SERVICE OPTIONS & PROGRAM DESIGN

Define the service. Make the handoffs clear.

Receiving and staging should support the next movement of the freight. We scope the facility arrangement, inbound checks, sorting work and holding period around the delivery or transportation program.

  1. 01

    Cross-dock and short-term staging

    Define the receiving window, freight profile, expected dwell time and the outbound handoff. Facility and space availability are confirmed for the program.

  2. 02

    Sort and route preparation

    Agree route grouping, labels, reconciliation, load sequence and who is responsible for dispatch-ready freight.

  3. 03

    Returns and reverse movements

    Set the receiving criteria, reason codes, disposition instructions and destination for returned or undelivered products.
