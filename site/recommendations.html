V02 design notes

Recommendations for chainexglobal.com

What changed in the V02 website proposal and why. Reviewed against the current public site.

See the V02 home pagePilot home
Recommendations

Prioritized changes

High priority

Lead with one clear promise

Keep "Capacity connected. People accountable." as the hero and cut the hero to one sentence plus two buttons (Request coverage, Apply). The current home page repeats the service list three times.

High priority

One sign-in for every portal

Replace "Partner login", "Team login" and "My portal" with one Sign in that routes by role (done in V02: auth/signin.html).

High priority

Put applications on the site, not behind a login

Driver, DSP, carrier and warehouse applications start from the public site and land in the onboarding queue with an owner and due date (V02: auth/apply.html).

High priority

Add a public tracking page

Shippers' customers look for tracking first. V02 adds track.html using the same data as dispatch.

Medium

Show proof, not adjectives

Replace generic claims with live numbers: on-time %, stations, service lines, coverage. Use the weekly scorecard as the source.

Medium

Separate audiences in the nav

Shippers, Carriers, Drivers & DSPs and Careers each get one page with one call to action.

Medium

Remove the loading screen

The current "Loading your next page…" interstitial delays every page; V02 pages load instantly.

Medium

Use real photography

Replace service illustrations with ChainEx photos of trucks, crews and stations (to be supplied).

Low

Day/night theme and accessibility

Auto theme by local sunrise/sunset, AA contrast, visible focus, and mobile-first layouts (V02).

Low

Keep USPS/government work off the main site

Government bids live only in the internal Bids workspace.
