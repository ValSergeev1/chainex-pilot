AN EXAMPLE OF THE SERVICE IN PRACTICE

A distribution operation connecting inbound and outbound work

An inbound lane can feed an agreed receiving window, followed by defined staging and local distribution. Each facility, truck and dispatch handoff has an established responsibility.

Illustrative configuration. Service availability and terms are confirmed for your program.

Before the first handoff

Provide the lane, schedule, pallet count, total weight and loading/unloading responsibilities. Include dock access, equipment restrictions and appointment procedures for both facilities.

When the plan changes

Late freight readiness, facility queues, appointment changes and load discrepancies can affect the plan. Define dispatch escalation, waiting-time treatment and who can authorize an alternate handoff.
