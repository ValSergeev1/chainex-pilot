SERVICE OPTIONS & PROGRAM DESIGN

Define the service. Make the handoffs clear.

A facility-to-facility move is more than a truck between two addresses. Loading readiness, appointments, trailer or truck configuration and receiving requirements all affect how a lane should be scheduled and priced.

  1. 01

    Scheduled facility connections

    Set origin and destination contacts, pickup windows, delivery deadlines, service days and the process for schedule changes.

  2. 02

    Box-truck or tractor-trailer capacity

    Review load size, pallet count, freight dimensions, total weight and facility access before confirming the equipment.

  3. 03

    Inbound-to-final-mile programs

    Coordinate the receiving handoff with downstream sorting, staging and route preparation where those services are available.

When the plan changes

Late freight readiness, facility queues, appointment changes and load discrepancies can affect the plan. Define dispatch escalation, waiting-time treatment and who can authorize an alternate handoff.
