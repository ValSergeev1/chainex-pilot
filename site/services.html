CONNECT THE SERVICES

A delivery program can start before the route.

A shipment may need an inbound facility transfer, a receiving handoff, sorting and staging, then a local delivery route. Those steps work better when the people responsible for each handoff share a defined plan.

We can review connected services together, including the facility arrangements, crew requirements, customer communication and return process. Each part of the program is confirmed against available capacity and the agreed scope.

    Receive & prepare

    Manifest, condition and staging requirements.

  2. 02
