SERVICE OPTIONS & PROGRAM DESIGN

Define the service. Make the handoffs clear.

White-glove delivery is a product-specific service, not a single bundle of unlimited extras. We build the appointment, handling, placement and agreed finishing tasks around the product and the customer’s location.

  1. 01

    Two-person placement

    Define the room and delivery path, product protection needs and the crew configuration. Include stairs, elevators and building access in the service plan.

  2. 02

    Assembly or selected installation

    Provide the make, model, instructions, tools and qualification requirements. Each task is reviewed and accepted separately; utility connections are not assumed.

  3. 03

    Packaging removal or haul-away

    Specify what needs to leave the property, how it will be handled and the agreed disposal or return arrangement.

Before the first handoff

Send product specifications, assembly instructions and a clear description of the site. Confirm whether the space is ready, who will receive the item and which finishing tasks the customer expects.

When the plan changes

Unexpected access problems, incomplete product kits, visible damage or work outside the accepted scope should be escalated before proceeding. Agree how the customer, dispatch and your team will resolve a changed delivery.

PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Before we get moving.

Does white glove include every installation?

No. The specific installation, tools, licensing, product requirements, and crew qualifications must be confirmed. Electrical, gas, and plumbing connections are not assumed.

How do you handle an unexpected access issue?

The crew coordinates with dispatch before changing the agreed scope. Additional handling or return arrangements require confirmation.
