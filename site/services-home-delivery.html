SERVICE OPTIONS & PROGRAM DESIGN

Define the service. Make the handoffs clear.

Home delivery starts with the product and the customer experience you want to provide. Parcels, bulky home goods and products requiring two-person handling need different equipment and procedures, but can be coordinated through one operating relationship.

  1. 01

    Parcel delivery

    Organize residential parcels around a receiving schedule, delivery territory, route plan and agreed delivery evidence.

  2. 02

    Bulky freight and home goods

    Define curbside, front-door or room-of-choice service, then match the vehicle, crew and appointment plan to the product and destination.

  3. 03

    White glove and two-person services

    Review inside placement, agreed assembly, selected installation or packaging removal as product-specific tasks with a defined scope.

AN EXAMPLE OF THE SERVICE IN PRACTICE

A retailer with a mixed delivery profile

Small parcels can follow a local route operation, while larger products receive a separately scoped handling and appointment plan. The service level is selected by shipment rather than assuming every order needs the same configuration.

Illustrative configuration. Service availability and terms are confirmed for your program.

Before the first handoff

Share the order mix, product dimensions, delivery ZIPs, service levels and expected volume. Separate standard doorstep orders from products requiring inside placement, appointments or additional crew handling.

When the plan changes

Agree customer-contact rules and how to handle damage, access issues, unsuccessful attempts or changes in service level. The agreed scope determines whether a reattempt, return or additional task can be accepted.

PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Before we get moving.

Are these services tied to a single city?

The service menu applies across the regional network. Available ZIPs, equipment, and facilities are confirmed for your program. Nationwide emergency cartage is coordinated separately.

How is a program priced?

Volume, density, equipment, handling, schedule, service area, and additional requirements determine the quote.

Can you work with our systems?

Manifest, label, API, EDI, and delivery-record requirements are reviewed during onboarding. An integration is confirmed before it is promised.
