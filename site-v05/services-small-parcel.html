SERVICE OPTIONS & PROGRAM DESIGN

Define the service. Make the handoffs clear.

Parcel delivery works best when receiving schedules, route density and customer communication are planned together. We review the shipment mix and territory so the local operation fits the daily volume and service promise.

  1. 01

    Recurring route programs

    Define receiving cutoffs, delivery days, ZIP coverage, parcel limits and the expected handoff between your facility and the delivery team.

  2. 02

    Peak and overflow support

    Review temporary route needs, expected duration, system onboarding and available drivers before committing additional volume.

  3. 03

    Attempts and returns

    Agree customer-contact rules, unsuccessful-attempt evidence, reattempt authorization and the destination for undelivered freight.

PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Before we get moving.

Can you take seasonal volume?

Temporary capacity can be scoped around available crews, equipment, route density, and onboarding. We confirm capacity before accepting volume.

What happens after a failed attempt?

Attempt rules, customer contact, reattempt pricing, and returns handling are agreed for the program. A failed attempt is documented and escalated through dispatch.
