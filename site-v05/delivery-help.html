Delivery Help

Start with the tracking link in your shipping or delivery notification. Have your order or shipment reference ready when contacting support.

Check the latest update in your delivery notification. If you did not receive a link, ask the seller for your shipment reference.

I missed my delivery. What should I do?

Check the delivery notice for next steps. Contact us with your reference if you need help with the next attempt.

My delivery is marked delivered but missing.

Check the delivery photo or notes if available, along with your usual delivery location. Contact support with your shipment reference and delivery date.

My shipment arrived damaged.

Keep the packaging and take photos of the item and packaging. Contact the seller for its claims process or ask ChainEx for help locating the shipment record.
