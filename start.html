Try this end to end

  1. Open Dispatch (P&D) and the Driver app as CX-PILOT-001 in two tabs of the same browser.
  2. Driver: go on duty, check in, then Review assignment → Review & edit route on map. Select pins and Move to start, use Tap to sequence, drag rows, skip a stop, add an address note, fix a pin, report a road issue, then Submit route changes.
  3. Dispatch: Avery's row shows ✎ edited with a summary and a road issue. Press Accept or Revert; the driver sees the result.
  4. DSP portal as DSP-EEX-01: accept the offered route, assign it to Nate Silva. Sign in to the driver app as CX-1188: his empty state turns into the route.
  5. Shipper portal as ONTRAC-SEA: open a delivered shipment to see tracking and POD, create an order, upload the sample manifest, export a report.
  6. Press Ctrl K anywhere to jump to a page, task or driver. Shift L cycles Auto / Day / Night. Appearance follows Seattle sunrise and sunset when set to Auto.

How to test

  1. Open Dispatch and the Driver app in two tabs of the same browser. They share state through local storage and update live.
  2. In the driver app, sign in with a sample Driver ID (table above), go on duty, check in, load-scan and complete a stop. Watch the task move on the desk.
  3. On the desk, switch lines in the top bar. Try Auto-assign, drag a task onto a driver, or drag one onto Jamal Price or Ben Okoye to see a blocked assignment with its reason.
  4. Shuttle: open the Trip board, press Run lane again, change a lane's standing driver. In the driver app, Start next trip has trailer and seal prefilled.
  5. Desk → Partners → DSP view: tender stops to Eastside Express, then assign its driver Nate Silva.
  6. Dashboard → Partner onboarding: apply as a Carrier, upload all, sign, set payouts, submit. All checks pass and it auto-approves. Use the simulation panel to make a check fail.
  7. Dashboard → Automation & security → +7 days twice: Valley Drayage's insurance expires, it's suspended, and Sam is blocked in Dispatch and the driver app.
  8. Switch Acting as to Owen (Finance) or Ari (Viewer) to see role gating.

Test tools & reset

  • Desk top bar: +15 min clock, Test tools (scenarios, simulate a driver, +7 days, export/import JSON, reset a line or everything).
  • Driver app frame: Restart app and Reset sample data.
  • Dashboard: Automation & security → Test tools (+1/+7 days, run expiry monitor, reset).
  • State is per browser. A reset in any app resets all of them.

Sample date: Nov 12, 2026 · Pacific time. Maps need internet for tiles and fall back to a schematic offline.

Appearance: Auto (default, Seattle sunrise–sunset), Day or Night. Shared across every app. Try ?theme=day, ?theme=night, ?theme=auto&now=13:00 or ?now=22:00.
