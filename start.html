ChainEx Pilot V03

A clickable copy of the whole ChainEx system, from the public website through sign-in and applications to every portal, with ChainEx branding everywhere. Everything runs in your browser with sample data: no real partner or person data, money is simulated, and nothing is sent. V01 and V02 are kept unchanged so you can compare or revert. New in V03: map-first driver app, new dispatch board, warehouse portal, DSP fleet app (live map + availability), phone dashboards, and the V03 color refresh. Apps install on your phone.

V03 · current buildV02 · previousV01
Try the driver ⇄ dispatch loop: open the driver app and the dispatch board in two tabs. Go on duty, tap 💬 Dispatch and pick "Too many stops". The bot fixes your route and the desk gets a ticket under Driver tickets; approve it and the driver sees the result.

Install the apps on your phone

Add the driver app, dispatch board, warehouse portal, DSP fleet app or dashboard to your home screen: full screen, own icon, works offline. Sample data only.
